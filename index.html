Good to know

A few things
you may wonder.

Still have a question? We are happy to talk through the details with you.

What is paint protection film?

It is a clear film applied to selected body panels to help protect the paint from everyday wear. Film types and their properties vary by product.

Can I choose which parts of my car to protect?

Yes. You can ask about specific panels or broader coverage. We will review your car's model and explain the proposed coverage before you decide.

How do I find out the price and expected timing?

Send us your car's make, model, year and the areas you are considering via WhatsApp. We will share the available details before confirming an appointment.

Is there a warranty on the film or installation?

Terms vary depending on the product and service. Please ask for the written warranty details for the specific film and installation before booking.
